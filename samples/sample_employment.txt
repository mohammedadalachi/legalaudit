EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This Employment Contract is entered into between ABC Technology Sdn. Bhd. ("the Company") 
and the Employee named below.

1. POSITION AND COMMENCEMENT
The Employee is employed as a Software Developer commencing 1 January 2025.

2. SALARY
The Employee shall receive a monthly salary of RM 3,500 payable at the end of each month.

3. WORKING HOURS
The Employee shall work 8 hours per day, Monday to Friday.

4. PROBATION
The Employee shall serve a probationary period of 6 months from the date of commencement.

5. ANNUAL LEAVE
The Employee is entitled to 12 days of annual leave per year.

6. NON-COMPETE
The Employee agrees that for a period of 24 months after the termination of employment, 
they shall not join any competitor or start any competing business in Malaysia or worldwide.

7. DEDUCTION
The Company reserves the right to deduct from the Employee's salary any losses, damages, 
or shortfalls caused by the Employee, including recovery of training loan up to 80% of monthly wages.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee shall keep all proprietary information and trade secrets strictly confidential 
during and after employment, with no time limitation.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of Malaysia.
